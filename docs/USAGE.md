# Everyday use

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## PC → phone

| What | How |
|---|---|
| What you just copied (text or image) | Press **Ctrl + Alt + C**, or click **Send to phone** in the window or the tray menu. The phone shows a notification — tap **Copy**. |
| Files you copied in Explorer | Press **Ctrl + Alt + C** — they're sent as files. |
| Something you type | Use the **Send to phone** box (Ctrl+Enter sends). |
| Photos, videos, any file | Click **Photos, videos & files…**, or drag them onto the window. |
| Pull the PC clipboard from the phone | Swipe down on the phone → **Copy from PC** tile. |

## Phone → PC

| What | How |
|---|---|
| What you just copied (text or image) | Swipe down → **Send to PC** tile. A popup appears on the PC — click **Copy**. |
| Selected text in any app | Select it → **⋮** → **Send to PC**. |
| Photos, videos, any file | **Photo / video** or **Any file** buttons in the app, or **Share → Send to PC** from the gallery or any other app. Several at once is fine. |
| Something you type | Type in the app's **Send to PC** box → **Send text**. |

## Received files

- **Phone:** they appear in the app's **Files** list and as a notification with **Open**, **Save to gallery** (or Downloads) and **Share**. Turn on *Save received photos & videos* to put them in the gallery automatically (`Pictures/ClipBridge`, `Movies/ClipBridge`).
- **PC:** they're saved to `Downloads\ClipBridge` (Settings → *Received files go to* → **Change…**) and listed under **Files** with **Open** and **Show in folder**.
- Big transfers show a progress bar with **Cancel** on both sides.

## History

Both apps show the last 5 items copied on either device, plus anything you pin.

- **Phone:** tap an item to copy it; long-press for *Pin*, *Send to PC*, *Open link* and *Delete*.
- **PC:** **Copy**, or **⋯** for *Send to phone*, *Open link*, *Save image…*, *Pin* and *Delete*.

## Switches

| Switch | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-sync to phone | PC or phone | Every PC copy (text or image) lands in the phone's clipboard automatically. |
| Auto-copy from phone | PC | Skip the Copy button — what the phone sends goes straight into the PC clipboard. |
| Auto-copy what the PC sends | Phone | Same, the other way round. |
| Save received photos & videos | Phone | Saves them to the gallery as they arrive. |
| Start with Windows | PC | Starts quietly in the tray. |
| Send-to-phone hotkey | PC | Choose another shortcut if Ctrl + Alt + C clashes with an app. |

> Android only lets apps read the clipboard while they're on screen. That's why phone → PC goes through the tile, the share menu or opening the app, while PC → phone can happen in the background.
